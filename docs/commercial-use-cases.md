# Commercial Use Cases

The public repository is the open-source core. Commercial work should happen in
the private/pro repository.

## Agent Readiness Audit

Buyer: AI startups, agencies, and internal AI platform teams.

Deliverable:

- evaluated public-safe or private traces;
- approval/refusal/uncertainty/tool-claim findings;
- remediation priorities;
- scoped readiness report.

## Regulated Agent Safety Report

Buyer: teams in finance, health, legal, enterprise IT, or customer operations.

Deliverable:

- private evidence review;
- standards coverage;
- residual risk summary;
- exportable executive report;
- private appendix for internal review.

## On-Prem Eval Lab Setup

Buyer: teams that cannot send traces to a hosted SaaS.

Deliverable:

- local evaluator setup;
- private adapters;
- reproducible report workflow;
- customer-specific policy/case pack;
- operator training.

## Product Boundary

Public repo:

- evaluator core;
- public-safe benchmark;
- schemas;
- deterministic checks;
- sample reports.

Private/pro repo:

- customer workflows;
- private evidence handling;
- proprietary case packs;
- polished dashboards;
- paid exports and templates.
